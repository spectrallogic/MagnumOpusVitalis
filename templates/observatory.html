How to read this instrument

Rings 01–04 advance 3° per completed scheduler cycle, with 180 ms interpolation. Ring 05 counts observed passes through the instrumented transformer layer, including unsteered and background passes; these are not GPU completion timings or generated-token counts. A clock cycle can include skipped or failed work; it is not a token count. The outer rim divides the last 256 recorded bus writes by source; thickness follows mean write magnitude.

Inner spokes show RMS magnitude in up to 48 contiguous groups of bus coordinates; brighter tips indicate a nonnegative mean. Their spherical positions are a fixed layout, not semantic distances. Core light follows bus magnitude. These are engine measurements, not the model’s full hidden activations.

The five sine tones are a listening convention: 110 / 165 / 220 / 330 / 440 Hz. Loudness follows the newly observed cycle rate. No new counts means no tone. Pause, hidden tabs, and lost telemetry mute the instrument. Reduced-motion settings disable rotation interpolation.
